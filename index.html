How we work

We're small on purpose. That shapes everything about how our apps are built and what they ask of you.

Privacy by default

Our apps have no user accounts and keep your data on your device. We collect the minimum a feature needs, and nothing more.

Straight about money

Some of our free apps show ads; our paid apps don't, and we say which is which up front. Either way we don't sell your data and we use no third-party analytics.

You reach actual people

There's no support ticket maze. Email us and one of the two people who built the app writes back, usually within two business days.
