Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback about a FindTime app? We read everything, and one of the two of us writes back.

support@findtime.net

We aim to reply within 2 business days.

To help us fix it faster, include:

  • Which app (for example BiteMatch) and its version number, shown in the app's Settings screen
  • Your device model and OS version (for example iPhone 15, iOS 26.5)
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one

Billing and subscriptions

Purchases are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so subscription changes and refunds go through them.

On iPhone or iPad

  • Manage or cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Request a refund: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restore a purchase: open the app's Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases.

On Android

  • Manage or cancel: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Request a refund: Google Play refund request.

Deleting an app does not cancel its subscription — cancel it in your store account first.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Our apps have no sign-up and no login. Your data lives on your device.

Why does the app want my location?

Only apps that need it ask, and only to answer the request in front of you — BiteMatch uses it to find restaurants near you. We don't store your coordinates or build any history of where you've been. You can decline or revoke access in your device settings at any time.

Do your apps have ads?

Some of our free apps are supported by ads; our paid and subscription apps are not. Each app's store listing says which it is. If you'd rather not see personalized ads, decline the tracking prompt on iOS or opt out of personalized ads on Android — you keep every feature either way. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Nearly everything our apps store is on your device and goes with it. Anything our servers briefly hold — like a shared swipe session — deletes itself within 24 hours. For anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

I found a bug or have a feature request

Email us. We're a two-person studio, so we can't build everything, but we do read every message and a lot of what ships started as someone's email.

Privacy requests

To ask about, access, or delete your information, or to opt out of personalized advertising, email support@findtime.net. We respond within 30 days. Our full Privacy Policy explains what we do and don't collect.
