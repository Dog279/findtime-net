Privacy Policy

FindTime · Effective August 1, 2026 · Last updated August 1, 2026

The short version: we don't sell your data and never have. Our apps have no user accounts and no third-party analytics. Most of what you do stays on your device. Some of our free apps show ads — section 7 explains exactly what that involves and how to opt out. Our paid apps have none.

1. Who we are and what this covers

FindTime ("FindTime", "we", "us") is a two-person app studio. This policy covers our website at findtime.net and every app we publish, including BiteMatch and Ashfall, unless a specific app publishes its own policy that says otherwise. New apps we release are covered by this policy from the day they ship.

The data controller is FindTime LLC, Davis, California, United States. You can reach us any time at support@findtime.net.

2. Our approach

We build small apps and we collect as little as each app actually needs. Concretely, across every FindTime app and this website:

  • We do not require or offer user accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, calendar, health data, or browsing history.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs in any app.
  • We do not sell your personal information. See section 8.
  • We do not use your data to train machine-learning models.
  • Some of our free apps are supported by advertising; our paid and subscription apps are not. Section 7 sets out exactly what an ad-supported app shares and how to turn personalized ads off.

3. What we collect, and why

Data that stays on your device

Most of what our apps remember never leaves your phone. This includes your preferences and settings, your in-app history and progress (for example your taste profile, swipes, and liked restaurants in BiteMatch, or your save file in Ashfall), and your trial or subscription state. We cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

findtime.net is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and embeds no trackers. Our web host records standard server request logs (including IP address) for security and reliability.

4. How we use data

We use the data above only to: provide and operate the features you asked for; validate and restore purchases; serve and measure advertising in our ad-supported apps; keep our services secure and prevent abuse; diagnose crashes and technical problems; and comply with law. We do not use it for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract to deliver the app features you request and to honor purchases; legitimate interests to keep our services secure, prevent abuse, and fix bugs; consent for device permissions such as location and for personalized advertising, which you grant in your device settings or in a consent prompt and can withdraw at any time; and legal obligation where we are required to retain records.

7. Advertising

Some FindTime apps are free and supported by advertising. Others are paid or subscription-based and contain no ads at all. Where an app shows ads, we say so on its App Store listing and in the app itself.

Apps with no advertising

As of the effective date above, BiteMatch and Ashfall contain no advertising and no advertising SDKs. Nothing in this section applies to them.

What an ad-supported app shares

In an ad-supported app, ads are delivered by [AD PROVIDER, e.g. Google AdMob]. To serve and measure them, the ad provider receives your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iOS or the Advertising ID on Android) where you have permitted its use, your IP address and coarse location, and basic device and app information such as device model, operating system version, and which app you are using. The ad provider uses this both on our behalf and for its own purposes under its own privacy policy.

We do not send advertisers your name, email address, contacts, precise saved location history, or the contents of your on-device app data such as your taste profile, saved items, or game saves.

Your choices

  • On iOS: before an app uses your advertising identifier to track you, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks your permission. If you decline, the identifier is not used to track you and you will see non-personalized ads instead. Change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Everywhere: declining personalized ads does not remove ads, and it does not reduce the app's functionality. You still get the app; the ads are just less targeted.
  • Removing ads: where an app offers a paid tier that removes advertising, buying it stops the advertising SDK from loading at all.

8. Selling and sharing your personal information

We have never sold personal information and we do not sell it, as "sell" is defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and comparable US state privacy laws.

Those same laws define "sharing" more narrowly, as disclosing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. In our ad-supported apps, the disclosure to our advertising provider described in section 7 may qualify as sharing when you have permitted personalized ads. You can opt out at any time by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS, by opting out of personalized ads on Android, or by emailing support@findtime.net. In apps without advertising, we do no sharing of any kind.

Apart from the service providers listed in section 6, we do not disclose personal information to third parties. We may disclose data if legally compelled to do so — for example in response to a valid subpoena or court order — or where necessary to protect our rights or someone's safety. Given how little we hold, there is usually very little to give.

9. Retention, deletion, and withdrawing consent

On-device data: delete the app and it goes with it. Apps that store a profile or save file also let you reset it from within the app's settings.

Shared-session data: deleted automatically within 24 hours of creation. You do not need to do anything.

Location: we don't retain it. You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, or Android Settings → Apps → Permissions. Location-dependent features stop working, but the rest of the app keeps running.

Advertising: withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time using the controls in section 7. Data already held by the ad provider is retained under its policy; the provider's opt-out and deletion tools are linked from its privacy policy.

Purchases: managed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, and retained as required to honor your subscription and meet our record-keeping obligations. Manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Accounts: our apps have no user accounts, so there is no account to delete. If we ever add accounts, we will provide in-app account deletion.

Anything else: email support@findtime.net and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict processing; to withdraw consent; to opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising; and to appeal a decision we make about your request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights — in particular, opting out of personalized ads never costs you features.

To exercise a right, email support@findtime.net. Please note an honest limitation: because we deliberately hold no information that identifies you, we usually cannot locate "your" data, and we may be unable to fulfill an access or deletion request beyond what deleting the app already accomplishes. We are not going to start collecting more data about you in order to verify a request.

EEA and UK users have the right to lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

11. App-specific details

BiteMatch

BiteMatch asks for your location for one purpose: finding restaurants near you. Your coordinates are sent to our server, which queries Google Places and returns nearby restaurants. We do not save your coordinates and we build no history of where you have been. Results are cached on your device for up to 24 hours.

When you create or join a shared swipe session, our server temporarily stores the session's restaurant list and each participant's likes and passes so both phones can see matches live. A session is visible only to its participants, is reachable only through the room code you choose to share, and is deleted automatically within 24 hours. No chat, contact, or identity information is involved. Your taste profile, swipe history, and liked restaurants stay on your device. BiteMatch contains no advertising.

Ashfall

Ashfall is entirely offline. It makes no network requests, has no backend, contains no advertising, and collects nothing. Your save file is stored on your device only.

12. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We do not knowingly serve personalized advertising to anyone under those ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email support@findtime.net and we will delete it.

13. International transfers

We are based in California, United States, and our service providers operate globally, so your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Access to our backend is restricted to the two of us. Our database enforces row-level security, so a shared session is readable only by its participants. No system is perfectly secure, but the strongest protection we can offer is that we hold almost nothing worth stealing.
